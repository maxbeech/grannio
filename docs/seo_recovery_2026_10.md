Audit only. No code or URL changes were made in this pass.

## Diagnosis

Search Console (sc-domain:grannio.com), impressions per day:

| Period | Impressions/day | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Aug - 31 Aug | about 115 | Includes the 18-21 Aug spam update window. 110-153/day through it, so no cliff on those dates. |
| 1 - 9 Sep | about 95 | |
| 10 - 25 Sep | about 45 | Step down starts 10 Sep. The noindex release for generic state and city pages shipped on 21 Sep. |
| 28 Sep - 18 Oct | about 47 (978 total) | Flat since. |

Clicks are tiny either way: 22 clicks in 1 Jul - 17 Aug, about 4 in the last three weeks. Average position moved from about 60 to about 40 while impressions fell, which means the lost impressions were mostly at positions 50-90.

There is no dated cliff on the spam update. The drop is a drift that starts 10 Sep. The most likely explanation is our own change: the generic city pages (1,486 impressions and 15 of the 22 clicks in the earlier window, nearly all at positions 6-10) and 36 non-statewide state pages were set to noindex on 21 Sep. Those pages are no longer eligible to show, so impressions fall by design. The 10-20 Sep part of the step is not explained by that release and may be ordinary volatility (a second spam update ran 24 Sep - 8 Oct). We cannot separate the two with the data available.

## Editorial collection

The 15 guides generated by `editorialGuide` (500/800 sq ft cost, cost per sq ft, detached cost, plans, floor plans, design ideas, checklist, permit guide, loans, ROI, Fannie Mae, mother-in-law suite, California 2026 update, prefab vs site-built) share one structure and one shared set of per-topic filler sections. They are careful and cite sources, but they contain few numbers and several overlap pages that already exist (for example `adu-building-permit-guide` and `adu-permit-process`, `detached-adu-cost` and `/cost/detached`). This is the nearest thing to a scaled-content risk on the site.

I did not act on it. They are not name swaps, they are about a month old, they did not cause the August to September drop, and they have no ranking history to protect or to blame. If they still earn nothing by mid-December, consolidate them: 301 each into its existing counterpart (permit guide to `adu-permit-process`, loans and Fannie Mae to `how-to-finance-an-adu`, ROI to `adu-rental-income-and-roi`, California update to `california-adu-rules-2026`, prefab review to `prefab-adu-cost`, cost pages to `how-much-does-an-adu-cost` or `/cost/{type}`), and fold the plans, floor plans and design pieces into one.
